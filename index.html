Account & sync

Sign in to keep the same data on your phone, iPad and computer.

How Sync works

  • Everything you enter is saved on this device straight away, even with no internet.
  • Nothing changes on its own. When you press Sync while online, the app combines this device's data with your account, brings in new notices and news, and installs any app update.
  • Press Sync on your other devices to get the same data there.
  • If the same contract was edited on two devices, the newest edit is kept. Deleted contracts stay deleted.

How it works

How to get started, and the rules the app uses to decide your residential status.

Getting started

  1. Install the app. Open the link while online, then add it to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen), or click Install on a PC. After that it opens without internet.
  2. Create your account. In Account, create an account with your email and a password, so your data is kept safe and reaches your other devices.
    Open Account
  3. Upload your seafarer profile. In Upload seafarer profile, upload your most recent Seafarer Profile PDF, downloaded from the DG Shipping e-Governance portal. Your ships, sea service and certificates are filled in for you.
    Upload seafarer profile
  4. Complete the pending details. In Pending details, fill in what the profile doesn't include: passport dates, sign-on and sign-off ports, company and fleet. If you don't have your passport dates now, tap Estimate passport dates, and update them later when you have the actual dates.
    Pending details
  5. Press Sync. Sync saves your data to your account and brings in notices, news and app updates. Nothing changes until you press it.
  6. Check your results. The Dashboard shows this year's NRI position and what to do next; Year results shows every financial year and a report for your CA.

Day count

Days outside India come from your passport dates (left India and arrived India) when you enter them. Without passport dates, the CDC sign-on to sign-off period is used.

For a voyage that starts or ends at an Indian port, the CDC period is excluded from your India stay under Rule 126 (FY 2025-26 and earlier) and Rule 8 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026 with s.6 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Keep India days at 181 or fewer: at least 184 days outside in a 365-day year, 185 in a leap year.

Resident or not

Resident if in India 182 days or more. An Indian citizen working abroad or as crew gets 182 days in place of the 60-day test.

If your Indian income (excluding foreign income) is over ₹15 lakh, 120–181 days in India plus 365+ days in the previous 4 years makes you resident (RNOR).

Deemed resident (RNOR): Indian citizen, Indian income over ₹15 lakh, and not liable to tax in any other country.

RNOR or ROR

A resident is RNOR if NRI in 9 of the 10 previous years, or in India 729 days or less in the 7 previous years. Otherwise ROR.

NRI: salary for service on a foreign-going ship is outside Indian tax, even when credited to an NRE account (CBDT Circular 13/2017).

RNOR: foreign income is taxed only if received in India. ROR: worldwide income including sea salary is taxable.
